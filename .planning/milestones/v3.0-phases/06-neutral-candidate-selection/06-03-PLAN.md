<objective>
Collapse the asymmetric `MAJOR_MAPPING`/`MINOR_MAPPING` alias subworkflows into ONE uniform per-candidate `TARGETED_MAPPING` fan-out over the long-format candidate channel (D-04 / REFSEL-03), carry `confirmation_status` per candidate with index-safe meta-key joins, rebuild the `.major.`/`.minor.` filename slot from `meta.candidate_rank` in config (T-1, the shim hinge), and update every HCVGLUE + SUMMARIZE feed channel in lockstep (T-2). Regenerate the workflow snapshot.

Purpose: This is the routing-tier realization of the neutral candidate model. It is the highest-risk plan because two distinct, easy-to-miss couplings (the config-driven filename slot in T-1, and the feed-channel `.mix()` halving in T-2) silently corrupt or halve Summary.csv if either is missed. Both are first-class tasks here. Implements REFSEL-03 and completes REFSEL-01/02 at the topology level. Depends on the long-format contract (Plan 01) and the `candidates` emit (Plan 02).

Output: rewritten routing in `workflows/hcvtyper.nf`, single-closure `ext.prefix` in `conf/modules_hcv.config`, regenerated `tests/default.nf.test.snap`.
</objective>
