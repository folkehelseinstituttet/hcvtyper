<objective>
Emit side of Phase 7 (ASUP-01). Add a dominance-neutral per-subtype "assembly support" roll-up to `bin/blast_parse.R`: for every subtype seen in the de novo contigs, summarize the single best contig (by `sc_length`, D-03) and carry THAT contig's BLAST % identity, BLAST alignment length, full contig length, and k-mer coverage (the four ASUP-01 metrics) into a new `*.assembly_support.csv`. Declare and stub that output in the BLASTPARSE module.

This is the dominance-neutral replacement for the §7 `major_ref`/`minor_ref` logic — but §7 stays UNCHANGED (D-04 legacy shim runs in parallel this phase). The genotype-level collapse and the candidate join happen in `bin/summarize.R` (Plan 02); this plan emits subtype grain only.

Purpose: makes de novo/BLAST evidence an independent per-genotype signal (ASUP-01), ready for Plan 02 to join to candidates. No threshold verdict is applied here (D-01 — that is Phase 8).
Output: new `*.assembly_support.csv` per sample, declared+stubbed in BLASTPARSE, plus an R subprocess-contract test.
</objective>
